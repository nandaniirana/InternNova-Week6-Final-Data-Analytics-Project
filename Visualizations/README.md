\# Data Visualizations



This folder contains the visualizations created for the InternNova Week 6 final data analytics project.



\## Visualizations Included



1\. \*\*Monthly Sales Trend\*\*

&#x20;  - Shows monthly sales performance over time.

&#x20;  - Helps identify changes and trends in sales activity.



2\. \*\*Sales by Product Category\*\*

&#x20;  - Compares total sales across Technology, Furniture, and Office Supplies.

&#x20;  - Technology generated the highest sales.



3\. \*\*Profit by Product Category\*\*

&#x20;  - Compares total profit across product categories.

&#x20;  - Technology generated the highest profit, while Furniture generated comparatively low profit.



4\. \*\*Sales by Region\*\*

&#x20;  - Compares sales performance across West, East, Central, and South regions.

&#x20;  - West generated the highest sales, while South generated the lowest.



5\. \*\*Discount vs Profit\*\*

&#x20;  - Examines the relationship between discount levels and profit.

&#x20;  - The analysis indicates a negative relationship between discount and profit.



\## Tools Used



\- Python

\- Pandas

\- Matplotlib

\- Seaborn



The visualizations were created from the cleaned Superstore sales dataset.
