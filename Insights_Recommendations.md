\## 1. Business Overview



The analysis evaluates sales and profitability performance across product categories, regions, sub-categories, and time. The objective is to identify strong-performing areas, profitability concerns, and opportunities for business improvement.



The analysis is supported by exploratory data analysis, statistical analysis, Python visualizations, and an interactive Power BI dashboard.



\---



\## 2. Key Business Insights



\### Insight 1 — Technology is the Leading Sales Category



Technology generated approximately \*\*0.84M in sales\*\*, making it the highest-performing product category.



\*\*Business meaning:\*\* Technology products represent a major contributor to overall revenue and should remain an important focus area.



\---



\### Insight 2 — Office Supplies Has the Lowest Category Sales



Office Supplies generated approximately \*\*0.73M in sales\*\*, making it the lowest-performing category by sales among the three major categories.



\*\*Business meaning:\*\* The category may require further analysis of product-level demand, pricing, and customer purchasing patterns.



\---



\### Insight 3 — Furniture Shows a Significant Profitability Concern



Furniture generated approximately \*\*20K in total profit\*\*, substantially lower than Technology and Office Supplies.



The analysis also identified \*\*Tables\*\* as a major concern, with approximately \*\*-17.75K in total profit\*\*.



\*\*Business meaning:\*\* Furniture profitability should be investigated at the sub-category level, particularly for loss-making products.



\---



\### Insight 4 — West is the Highest-Sales Region



The \*\*West region\*\* generated approximately \*\*0.74M in sales\*\*, making it the strongest region by sales.



\*\*Business meaning:\*\* The West region demonstrates strong market performance and can provide useful practices or customer patterns that may be evaluated for other regions.



\---



\### Insight 5 — Sales Show Strong Monthly Fluctuation



Monthly sales vary considerably throughout the analysis period. Sales reached their highest observed level at approximately \*\*98K in December 2025\*\*.



\*\*Business meaning:\*\* The business experiences noticeable seasonal or monthly variation, which can support better sales planning and resource allocation.



\---



\## 3. Recommendations



\### Recommendation 1 — Review Pricing and Discount Strategy for Loss-Making Products



The negative profitability observed for the Tables sub-category indicates a need to review pricing, discount levels, product costs, and order-level profitability.



\*\*Recommended action:\*\*

\- Review discount levels applied to Tables.

\- Identify products with repeated negative margins.

\- Evaluate pricing and cost structure.

\- Set appropriate discount limits for low-margin products.



\---



\### Recommendation 2 — Strengthen High-Performing Technology Sales



Technology is the strongest category by sales and should be supported through continued product availability, targeted promotions, and customer-focused sales strategies.



\*\*Recommended action:\*\*

\- Maintain adequate inventory for high-demand Technology products.

\- Identify the highest-performing Technology sub-categories.

\- Use successful sales strategies from Technology to evaluate opportunities in weaker categories.



\---



\## 4. Overall Business Conclusion



The analysis shows that the business has a strong overall sales position, with approximately \*\*2.33M in total sales\*\* and \*\*292.30K in total profit\*\*.



Technology and the West region are important revenue contributors, while Furniture—particularly Tables—requires closer profitability monitoring.



The findings demonstrate that combining sales, profitability, regional, category, sub-category, and time-based analysis can support more informed business decisions.



\---



\## 5. Decision Summary



| Area | Finding | Business Action |

|---|---|---|

| Technology | Highest sales category | Sustain and strengthen performance |

| Office Supplies | Lowest category sales | Investigate demand and product performance |

| Tables | Negative total profit | Review pricing, costs and discounts |

| West | Highest regional sales | Maintain performance and identify transferable practices |
